Our story

VEXAMIA is an original animated entertainment world, built from scratch — its own characters, its own city, its own rules. Every story is made to be easy to jump into and hard to predict: comedy, adventure and cinematic moments that anyone, anywhere, can enjoy.

At the heart of it are VEX & MIA. Vex is chaos and impulse; Mia is clever and warm. Together they turn an ordinary day in their neon city into something unexpected — and they are just getting started.
